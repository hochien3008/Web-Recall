Privacy First, Always

Recall is built from the ground up as a 100% local-first AI memory layer. Your photos and memories never leave your device.

100% On-Device

Zero Cloud Upload

All OCR text recognition and AI semantic embeddings execute entirely on your phone's processor. No server ever sees your photos.

Smart Protection

3-Level Text Guard

  • Ordinary: Useful notes & text indexed locally in SQLite.
  • Sensitive: Financial, ID & contact data are redacted before saving.
  • Secret: Passwords, OTPs & PINs are instantly discarded from memory.
Original Integrity

Your Photos Stay Intact

Recall only references system photo identifiers. Deleting an item in Recall never alters or deletes your original camera roll.

Help & Support

Need assistance or have questions about Recall? We're here to help.

Does Recall require an internet connection?

No. Recall operates completely offline. Photo scanning, text extraction, local search, and reminders do not need Wi-Fi or cellular data.

How do I get started with AI semantic search?

Once you open Search in the app, you can opt-in to download the compact on-device AI model (multilingual-e5-small) once, after which it runs 100% locally.
